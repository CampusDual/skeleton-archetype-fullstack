# Requirements

## Functional Requirements

### FR-01

The system shall manage inventory products.

### FR-02

The system shall store stock levels.

### FR-03

The system shall manage recipes.

### FR-04

The system shall automatically consume stock based on sales.

### FR-05

The system shall generate replenishment alerts.

### FR-06

The system shall recommend replenishment quantities.

### FR-07

The system shall calculate dish availability.

### FR-08

The system shall integrate with POS systems.

### FR-09

The system shall expose operational dashboards.

### FR-10

The system shall maintain a historical inventory movement log.
